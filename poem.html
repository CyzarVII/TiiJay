A poem about us

Black and purple, quiet and soft a small poem from my heart to yours.

"In the quiet, it's you"
In a world that moves too quickly, you walk in at your own pace, and somehow, Tiana, my heart learns how to breathe slower whenever you are near. You are the calm between my thoughts, the warmth inside my chest, the soft "it's okay" I feel even when no one says the words out loud. With you, ordinary moments become stories, car rides feel like scenes, and simple days feel like reasons to be grateful I woke up again. If love is a language, yours is spoken in gentle looks, in shared jokes, in the way you listen and love without needing a stage. And if I get to spend my tomorrows learning you, your moods, your dreams, your light, then every day from here on out will already be better than anything I imagined. Because in the quiet, when the noise is gone and the world feels small, it's still you, Tiana, it's always you.
— Jamal
Made in black & purple, for Tiana, from Jamal. No audience. No likes. Just us. 💜
